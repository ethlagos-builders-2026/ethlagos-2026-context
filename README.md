# ETH Lagos 2026 Context

Context engineering and research repository for our two-person ETH Lagos 2026 hackathon team.

This repository exists to collect the hackathon facts, tracks, technologies, prizes, partners, rules, deadlines, and build inspiration before we choose what to build.

## Important

No final project has been selected in this repository.

The goal at this stage is to understand the hackathon well enough that both teammates can research from the same source of truth.

## Event snapshot

- Event: ETH Lagos 2026
- Theme: Ethereum for all
- Dates: October 8–10, 2026
- Location: Lagos, Nigeria
- Venue: Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and Creative Arts, National Theatre Nigeria, Iganmu
- Public hackathon prize pool: $50,000 in ETH
- Hackathon kickoff: October 8, 4:30 PM
- Building period: October 8–10
- Public timeline lists Demo Day: October 10, 3:30 PM

## Hackathon challenges

1. Agents for the Real World
2. The "Invisible Crypto"
3. Sovereign Finance & Local RWAs
4. Decentralized Society

## Research status

Facts are separated into three categories:

- CONFIRMED: published by ETH Lagos or the relevant Ethereum standard.
- CONTEXT: interpretation that helps us understand what a challenge is asking for.
- OPEN: not published or not yet verified.

We should update this repository when the organizers publish sponsor bounties, judging criteria, final submission instructions, or other rules.

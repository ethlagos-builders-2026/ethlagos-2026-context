# Hackathon Overview

Snapshot date: October 6, 2026.

## CONFIRMED: Event

ETH Lagos 2026 runs from October 8–10, 2026 in Lagos, Nigeria.

The event describes its mission as bridging the gap between blockchain's promise and the reality of Africa's digital economy.

The broader event includes talks, technical workshops, community activities, and a hackathon.

## CONFIRMED: Venue

Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and Creative Arts  
National Theatre Nigeria  
Iganmu, Lagos

The public site lists dedicated hackathon space and high-speed internet among the venue facilities.

## CONFIRMED: Hackathon positioning

ETH Lagos says the hackathon focuses on the "how" while the main stage discusses the "why."

The organizer's wording is important:

> Builders are challenged to synthesize frontier technology with real Nigerian utility.

This means the hackathon is not presented as a generic Ethereum coding contest. Its public framing emphasizes useful products connected to Nigerian economic, consumer, infrastructure, privacy, and social needs.

## CONFIRMED: Four wider conference themes

### The UX Revolution
Making Ethereum invisible to the end user.

Technologies mentioned:
- ERC-4337
- Paymasters / gasless UX
- intents
- mobile-first design

### L2 & Agent Economy
Affordable rails for humans and autonomous machines.

Technologies mentioned:
- rollups
- the Superchain
- AI agents
- ERC-8004
- low-cost blobs

### Real-World Ethereum
Ethereum beyond trading and speculation.

Areas mentioned:
- Nigerian asset tokenization
- ZK identity
- advanced DeFi primitives

### Cypherpunk Lagos
Decentralization and privacy at scale.

Areas mentioned:
- decentralized social
- privacy tools
- zero-knowledge proofs
- quadratic funding

## CONFIRMED: Participant benefits

The public site lists:

- mentor access

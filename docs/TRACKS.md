# Hackathon Tracks

This document explains the four official ETH Lagos hackathon challenges without selecting a final project.

## Challenge 1: Agents for the Real World

### CONFIRMED organizer wording

"Building autonomous AI agents that perform useful economic labor on L2s (leveraging ERC-8004)."

### CONTEXT

The important parts of the challenge are:
- autonomous agents
- useful economic labor
- L2 execution
- ERC-8004

ERC-8004 is an Ethereum standard proposal for discovering agents and establishing trust through identity, reputation, and validation registries.

The standard itself does not define payments. Payments are described as orthogonal to ERC-8004.

### What this track appears to invite

Examples of the design space:
- agents that coordinate economic tasks
- agents that transact under defined permissions
- service agents
- agent marketplaces
- agent reputation systems
- agent validation systems
- agent-to-agent coordination on L2s

These are examples, not our selected project.

## Challenge 2: The "Invisible Crypto"

### CONFIRMED organizer wording

"Creating UX so clean grandma can use it (leveraging AA & Paymasters)."

### CONTEXT

AA means Account Abstraction.

ERC-4337 allows smart contract accounts to define programmable validation and use UserOperations instead of requiring the user to manage every normal transaction directly.

Paymasters can sponsor transaction gas or apply policy around how gas is paid.

The track is therefore strongly connected to:
- gasless interactions
- smart accounts
- onboarding without crypto-native complexity
- mobile-first flows
- hiding chain/network details from ordinary users
- reducing repeated approvals and transaction steps

### What this track appears to invite

Examples of the design space:
- consumer payments
- remittances
- savings/contribution products
- merchant checkout
- onboarding flows
- smart-wallet applications
- applications where users do not need to hold ETH for gas

These are examples, not our selected project.

## Challenge 3: Sovereign Finance & Local RWAs

### CONFIRMED organizer wording

"Bridging on-chain liquidity with off-chain Nigerian assets."

### CONTEXT

RWA means Real-World Asset.

This challenge points toward financial products where an off-chain asset, obligation, revenue stream, or claim has a meaningful onchain lifecycle.

The event's wider "Real-world Ethereum" theme also mentions:
- tokenizing Nigerian assets
- ZK identity
- advanced DeFi primitives

### What this track appears to invite

Examples of the design space:
- invoice/receivable financing
- tokenized business obligations
- agricultural or warehouse-linked assets
- asset-backed financing
- local investment or liquidity rails
- verified real-world cash-flow products

These are examples, not our selected project.

---

## Challenge 4: Decentralized Society

### CONFIRMED organizer wording

"Building censorship-resistant social tools and privacy tech tailored for Lagos."

### CONTEXT

The wider "Cypherpunk Lagos" theme also names:
- decentralized social
- privacy tools
- ZK
- quadratic funding

This challenge is broader than social media. It includes community, civic, identity, privacy, coordination, and public-goods use cases where decentralization has a clear reason to exist.

### What this track appears to invite

Examples of the design space:
- privacy-preserving credentials
- decentralized community tools
- censorship-resistant publishing/reporting
- private eligibility proofs
- quadratic funding
- community governance
- selective disclosure systems

These are examples, not our selected project.

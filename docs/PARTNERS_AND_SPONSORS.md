# Partners and Sponsors

Snapshot date: October 6, 2026.

The live ETH Lagos public page currently lists the following partners.

## Platinum partners

- Ethereum Foundation
- Polygon

## Gold partners

- Aave
- Uniswap
- Optimism

## Silver partners

- Chainlink
- The Graph
- ENS
- Gitcoin

## Important distinction

A partner listing does not automatically mean there is a hackathon bounty requiring that protocol.

The public page confirms that sponsor bounties and prizes exist, but it currently does not publish a sponsor-by-sponsor bounty table.

## Useful research surfaces

These partners suggest areas that may appear in workshops, mentorship, ecosystem support, or bounties:

- Polygon: EVM scaling / application execution
- Optimism: L2 and Superchain ecosystem
- Arbitrum: L2 and DeFi ecosystem
- Aave: lending and liquidity
- Uniswap: swaps and liquidity
- Chainlink: oracle/data/interoperability infrastructure
- The Graph: indexing and onchain data
- ENS: identity and naming
- Gitcoin: public goods and funding mechanisms

This section is context only. We should not force any integration before official bounty requirements are known.

# Open Questions

Questions to resolve from organizers, official registration material, sponsor bounty pages, or kickoff briefings.

## Submission

1. What is the exact submission deadline?
2. What platform will submissions use?
3. Is a public GitHub repository required?
4. Is a live deployment required?
5. Is a demo video required?
6. Are contract addresses and transaction links required?

## Build rules

7. Must all project-specific code be written after kickoff?
8. Are starter kits and personal boilerplates allowed?
9. Are existing open-source libraries allowed without restriction?
10. What is the official team-size limit?

## Judging

11. What is the judging rubric?
12. How much weight goes to product utility, technical implementation, innovation, design, and presentation?
13. How long is the final pitch/demo?
14. Is there a Q&A period?

## Tracks and sponsors

15. Can one project enter multiple challenges?
16. What sponsor bounties are active?
17. What are the sponsor-specific requirements?
18. Are sponsor prizes stackable with challenge/main prizes?
19. Which networks are eligible for each sponsor bounty?

## Event logistics

20. What is the authoritative Day 3 schedule?
21. When are AWS and Infura credits distributed?
22. Will there be official mentor office hours for each challenge?
23. Will the promised full schedule PDF be published before kickoff?
